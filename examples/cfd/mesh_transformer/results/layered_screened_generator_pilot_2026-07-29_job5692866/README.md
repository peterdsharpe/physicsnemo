# Path-ordered local-generator pilot

This directory preserves the underpowered 500-step signal check for the
registered path-ordered composition study.

The pilot is not a scientific verdict. The analytic oracle was exact, and the
sorted learned generator predicted exactly zero layer-order contrast as
required. The two physical-order seeds recovered 93% and 94% of the true
contrast and reduced paired field error by factors of 4.4--5.1 relative to the
pooled ordered model. They also improved interpolation, held-out modes, and
low-coefficient transfer strongly.

High-coefficient transfer remained unresolved: the physical-order seeds had
12%--14% error, similar to or worse than the undertrained pooled control. The
registered five-seed, 4,000-step comparison is required to separate successful
ordered composition from extrapolation of the learned local constitutive law.

- `pilot/` contains the six signal-check reports.
- `STATUS` records successful terminal completion.
